First Impressions Start With Sound

People are always surprised to learn I’m not a native speaker, and that I’ve acquired my British accent without living in the UK.

  • "You're inspiring. By the way, when you started speaking I thought you were a native speaker and that's the best compliment a non-native speaker can ever get. Your level of accent acquisition is phenomenal."

    — An Australian teacher at a prestigious Moscow school said.

  • “How long have you been living in England?”

    — My student from Iran asked me when I was teaching a CELTA course in London. The look on his face when I replied, "Actually, never" was priceless. Apparently, I sounded British enough to have lived there for years!

  • "I was sure you were from England, but when I looked closer, I thought… hmmm…. she looks Russian… You have such beautiful British pronunciation!"

    — A girl from Kazakhstan told me when I met her at a hotel in China. That interaction made me realize how pronunciation shapes perceptions.

  • "Here is the English menu."

    — The waiter in Turkey announced with a confident smile. He clearly assumed it was the only option for me. He had no idea I’d have much preferred the Russian one — which, of course, they also had!
